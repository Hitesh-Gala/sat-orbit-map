Chinese Satellite Series — Field Guide

A program-by-program survey of the People's Republic of China's on-orbit satellite families — what each series does, who operates it, the orbit it works from, and where it stood as of mid-2026. Compiled from open-source launch trackers, operator releases, and space-press reporting. For live positions of every active PRC payload, see the China Sat Repo.

How to read this guide. Designations follow the Chinese program name (with pinyin / Hanzi where useful) and the common Western catalogue label. "Status" reflects the most recent publicly reported activity as of mid-2026; highlighted text marks the freshest 2025–2026 data point. Many higher-numbered Yaogan, Gaofen and TJS craft are dual-use or classified, so role descriptions for those are best open-source assessments, not official statements.
